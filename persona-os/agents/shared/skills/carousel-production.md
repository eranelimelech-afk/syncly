Produce a slide plan: 1 cover + up to 9 slides, all 4:5.

- Cover carries the hook; the last slide carries the ending (question or cut).
- One clue per post across all slides combined, not per slide.
- Each slide names its reference angle and wardrobe row.
- Caption in the persona's voice, English, almost no emoji, few hashtags.
